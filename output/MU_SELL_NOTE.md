## Habits
1. **I decide on the closing price,** not mid-day swings.
2. **I don't buy more** to "average down" while it's below my price.
3. **After I sell, I don't buy back the same day.**
4. **Earnings week:** Micron reports about every 3 months (next around mid/late December). The stock has moved about ±9% the day after a report. I decide before that date whether to hold through it.
5. **Taxes:** profit on shares held under a year is taxed like salary (about 29% for me). I keep that money aside.
6. **LITE and MU both ride the AI boom and can fall together.** My −20% limit applies to the two of them combined.
