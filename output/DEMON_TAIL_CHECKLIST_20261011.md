2. **Your pot:** decide the amount now (e.g. **$3,500**) and the two hard limits:

   - **Stop for the week at −$1,500 total.**
3. **Discord alerts on your phone:**
   - Optionality server → **#demon-alerts** → bell icon → **All Messages**.
   - Phone Settings → Discord → Notifications **on**, with sound. Allow Discord through Focus / Do Not Disturb.
4. **Robinhood watchlist:** add his usual tickers so they're one tap away: **SPY, QQQ, TSLA, NVDA, MSFT, COIN, QCOM**.
5. **Practice once:** build an order without submitting it. Ticker → **Trade** → **Trade Options** → **Buy** + **Call** → pick today's date → tap a strike → Next. Look at the bid/ask, then back out.

## Morning (9:10–9:30 AM ET)
- He often posts "SPY call on watch" or "TSLA call on watch" before the open.
- When he does, open that ticker's option chain for **today's expiration** so you're ready.

## When an alert hits (target: under 60 seconds)
Example alert: "BTO $SPY 772c 10/05 @0.75". That means buy to open: ticker SPY, 772 strike, call, expiring 10/05, his price $0.75.

1. **Find it:** ticker → Trade Options → **Buy** → **Call** (or **Put** if it says "p") → the expiration → the strike.
2. **Order type: Limit.** Never use Market; spreads on these are wide.
3. **Limit price:** the current **ask**, but **no more than 20% above his posted price** ($0.75 × 1.2 = **$0.90**).
   - If the ask is above that, **skip the trade**.
   - Followers paid 19% above his price on average, and chasing makes it worse.
4. **Quantity** for $300 a trade:

   | Option price | Contracts | Cost |
   |---|---|---|
   | $0.40–0.50 | 6 | $240–300 |
   | $0.51–0.60 | 5 | $255–300 |
   | $0.61–0.75 | 4 | $244–300 |
   | $0.76–1.00 | 3 | $228–300 |
   | $1.01–1.50 | 2 | $202–300 |
   | over $1.50 | 1 or skip | |

5. **Submit.** If it doesn't fill within ~1 minute, **cancel**. Don't raise the price.

## Right after it fills ("leave it in Robinhood")
- **Set your exit:** open the position → **Sell** → **Limit** → your target price → time in force **Good 'til canceled** → submit. It then sells on its own if the price gets there, even while you're at work.
- **Pick ONE target and keep it all week:**

  | Target (on his last 3 weeks of alerts) | $300-per-alert result |
  |---|---|
  | Your fill × 1.3 (+30%) | about −$2,900 |
  | Your fill × 1.5 (+50%) | about −$3,300 |

  - **Holding to expiry** was about −$2,100, but it means watching 0DTE trades go to zero.
  - **None of these was positive.** Selling right at his final brag was positive, but that's only knowable afterward.
- **Same-day (0DTE) trades:** if your target isn't hit, the option can expire worthless at 4 PM. Robinhood may also close expiring positions itself late in the day.
- **Only one sell order per position.** Robinhood holds those contracts for it, so cancel it first if you want to sell another way.
- **Don't average down** when he does ("brought my avg to…"). It doubles the bet on a trade that's already losing.
