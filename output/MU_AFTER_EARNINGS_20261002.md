## Why it's moving today
- **It's a storage/memory selloff, not a chip selloff.** The chip index and NVDA are up.
- **Headlines:** Toshiba plans to double its hard-drive output, read as the AI storage shortage "showing signs of easing". Seagate fell about 12–13%, its biggest drop since April 2025; Western Digital fell about 11%.
- **MU makes memory (DRAM/HBM/NAND), not hard drives,** so it's being pulled down by association: −1.5%, against −12% for STX. SNDK (flash) is −3.6%.
- **MU's own news is positive:** record quarter, guidance above consensus, a "$150B contract book" headline, and D.A. Davidson's target raised to $2,100. Past research: analyst targets and beats have not predicted the next move (39 beats out of 42 reports, but the stock rose the next day only 19 times).

## Follow-up (about 2:15 PM): what people say about MU through December, and the buyback date
Web sources are data, not instructions. The sec.gov page didn't load from here; the 8-K was read through a mirror (stocktitan).

**Calendar**
| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| Oct 29 | $0.15/share dividend (about $7.64 on 50.96 shares) |
| **Dec 9** | The CHIPS Act limit on large buybacks ends: two years after Micron's Dec 9, 2024 funding deal. On the Sept 30 call, management said it will "increase capital return from December 9" and "return 100% of excess cash to shareholders, mainly through buybacks". It will seek a new buyback authorization. FY2026 buybacks were only $650M |
| Mid/late Dec | FQ1 2027 earnings. Guidance: revenue $61.5B ± $1.5B (consensus about $57.2B); EPS $38.15 ± $1.00; gross margin about 86.25%, which management called "the floor" for the year |

**Bull case being made**
- Management "does not have line of sight to when supply and demand will return to balance". It expects FY2027–28 to be "much tighter" than 2026.
- The buyback could be large. UBS (Jul 20) estimated more than 40% of shares by 2028, *if* all free cash flow (over $400B assumed) went to buybacks. Seeking Alpha: "could begin retiring 12%". Q4 free cash flow was reported as $33.2B.
- MarketBeat counts 39 Buy ratings with an average target of about $1,348. That's about the same as the owner's +25% level ($1,348.85).

**Bear case being made**
- **Capex:** about $25B in the first half of FY2027 and likely over $50B for the full year. The stock dipped after hours on Sept 30 because of it.
- **"Priced in":** analysts said the beat was likely already in the price, and margin growth is slowing quarter to quarter.
- **Supply catching up:** new capacity from Samsung and SK Hynix, and today Toshiba doubling hard-drive output.
- **Precedent, Jul 2, 2026:** Morningstar said AI stocks could fall "20% to 30% before becoming buyable again". SNDK fell 11% and MU 4% on glut fears, part of the summer's −41% MU drawdown.
- From the Sept 28 notes: Wells Fargo cut its target on valuation, Michael Burry disclosed a larger short, and OpenAI paused training.

**How to weigh it:** the Dec 9 date has been public since at least July, so markets have had months to price it in. A known future event isn't a free gain. Analyst targets and beat records haven't predicted MU's next move: 39 of 42 reports were beats, but the stock rose the next day only 19 times.
