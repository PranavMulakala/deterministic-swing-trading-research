## Why it's moving today
- **It's a storage/memory selloff, not a chip selloff.** The chip index and NVDA are up.
- **Headlines:** Toshiba plans to double its hard-drive output, read as the AI storage shortage "showing signs of easing". Seagate fell about 12–13%, its biggest drop since April 2025; Western Digital fell about 11%.
- **MU makes memory (DRAM/HBM/NAND), not hard drives,** so it's being pulled down by association: −1.5%, against −12% for STX. SNDK (flash) is −3.6%.
- **MU's own news is positive:** record quarter, guidance above consensus, a "$150B contract book" headline, and D.A. Davidson's target raised to $2,100. Past research: analyst targets and beats have not predicted the next move (39 beats out of 42 reports, but the stock rose the next day only 19 times).
