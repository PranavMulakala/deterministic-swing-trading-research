## Through the lens of what we've already learned
- **Breakouts:** our test #3 (a simple breakout on 5,259 stocks) failed with a profit factor of 0.94. **Video 7 adds four things that test lacked:** a market-regime filter, sector leadership, risk-based sizing, and a let-winners-run exit (trim + 10-SMA trail). Those are exactly the ingredients the momentum literature says matter, so it deserves its own fair test rather than a "breakouts already failed".
- **Opening-range and candle-sweep methods** (videos 1, 2, 5, 9): rules mostly clear and testable on minute data (QuantConnect has it, free tier). Published ORB studies fail after costs, so the bar is high. Video 2's "85–90%" is easy to check.
- **Options** (videos 6, 8): buying 0DTE calls is a lottery with occasional big winners shown on TikTok. Selling cash-secured puts is a sensible "get paid to wait" tool when you'd buy anyway. **Restrictions:** no LITE options (Lumentum policy). MU or SPY puts are allowed but need Robinhood options approval, and assignment means buying 100 shares (MU at ~$1,000 = ~$100k per contract; SPY ~$77k).
- **Every hook claim** ("10k a month", "85–90%", "$842k year", "+170%") is unverified. Several videos funnel to paid communities or streams.
