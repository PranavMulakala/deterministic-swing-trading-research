## What he does
- **His format:** posts buy alerts in #trade-ideas, like "$PLTR 207.5 CALL 10/16 @ 2.71", then follow-ups like "UP 114% AT 5.80".
- **Style:** short-dated stock and SPY calls, plus one longer GLD swing.
- **Risk rule:** "no stop losses," sizing the position as the loss you accept.
- **His pitch:** it's free.
  - "I do this for free, just post your gains in chat."
  - "I want to remain free."
  - "Ill put together a course or something within the next few weeks."
  - "I will do mentorships probably one day."
- **Background he gave:**
  - "trading 7-8 years" (Sept 29), then "6-7 years, lost my first year" (Oct 9);
  - started with $2K;
  - has a TikTok.
- **Server age:** about 3.5 weeks. There was no #trade-ideas alert until Sept 25.

## What this shows
1. **The entries are real.** Every alert price matched the market within a few cents, so he isn't inventing fills after the fact.
2. **Losers go quiet.** He posted +72%, +118%, +55% and +114% updates, but **nothing** on JBLU (−92%) or MCRI (−91%).
   - Members asked about MCRI and JBLU, and asked him to "post on whenever you exit." No exit posts were made.
   - The channel reads as 4 wins out of 4. The real record is 4 wins, 2 near-total losses and 1 open.
3. **The gains need perfect timing.** Without exit instructions, a follower who held lost **−$3,236 on $7,000**. Three of his four "winners" gave back most or all of the gain within days (SCSC 0.95→0.10, SPCX 5.60→0.48, SPY 2.41→0.36).
4. **The +$30,900 screenshot is a peak-minute mark, not a sale.**
   - The PLTR call touched $5.80 for one minute at 3:54 PM, with only 75 contracts trading.
   - His "UP 114% AT 5.80" post came at 3:56, when it was already $5.50–5.60.
   - It's real at that instant, but 100 contracts couldn't have been sold at $5.80.
   - The screenshot shows market value, not a closed trade.
5. **The funnel is classic.**
   - It's free now. Members are asked to post gains, which builds social proof.
   - A course is promised "within the next few weeks," and "mentorships one day."
   - Members are already offering money: "id pay few thousand for a mentorship"; one asked about his "vip group."
   - **This is the same path test #28's OWLS followed before it charged ($63 per 4 weeks).**
6. **The chat is risky advice from strangers.**
   - Members say "Just full port every alert play" and "full port Edward plays."
   - People recount losing $3K, $4K, "60k at 18," and "1500 to 40k then lost it all."
   - There were raids by fake-Discord promoters, and members asked for moderators repeatedly.
7. **The sample is tiny.** Seven alerts in about 2.5 weeks can't tell skill from luck.

## Verdict
- **Not a scam in the sense of fake trades,** and there's no payment today.
- **The track record is presented selectively:** losers go silent and gains are quoted at the peak minute.
- **As a follower,** results depend on exits he doesn't give. Holding lost about 46% of the money put in; selling at his exact peak posts made about +15%.
- **It's the same picture as OWLS (test #28):** what's posted looks far better than what a follower gets.
- If he launches a paid course or VIP, judge it by a full record that includes the losers.

**If you want to keep watching it:** log each alert (time, price, contract) and score it after the fact like this, without trading it. After 30+ alerts, the record will tell you whether there's an edge.
