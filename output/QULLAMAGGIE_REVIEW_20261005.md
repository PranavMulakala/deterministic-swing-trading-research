## Who he is
- **Background:** a Swedish swing trader. He started day trading in 2011 at 23, blew up his account 3–4 times in the first two years, and his first profitable year was 2013.
- **Financial independence in 2017.** He then shared free content on YouTube, Twitter and Twitch streams. He says he makes his money trading and doesn't sell courses ("help, not prey").
- **Reported record:** about $9,100 in May 2013 to roughly $80M+ by early 2021.
  - Reported among Sweden's top income earners in 2021. Swedish income tax records are public, which makes his story more checkable than most.
  - **But the account figures are self-reported and not audited.**
  - He reportedly gave back a large part of the peak (on the order of $60M) while heavily long into the 2022 bear market.

## The 3 setups, in plain words
**His risk rules** (the part beginners skip):
- Most positions are 10–20% of the account, and never more than 30% in one stock overnight.
- He risks only **0.25–1% of the account per trade**.
- **Small losses, big winners:** he says you can be very profitable with a 25–30% win rate if winners are 5–20× the risk.

1. **Breakouts**
   - **Find leaders:** scan for the top 1–2% of stocks by gains over 1, 3 and 6 months.
   - **Setup:**
     - a 30–100%+ move within the last 1–3 months;
     - then an orderly pullback and tight sideways range for 2 weeks to 2 months, riding the 10- and 20-day averages;
     - then a breakout.
   - **Entry and exits:**
     - **Entry:** above the opening-range high (first 1-, 5- or 60-minute candle).
     - **Stop:** the day's low, never wider than the stock's average daily range.
     - **Exits:** sell ⅓–½ after 3–5 days, move the stop to break-even, then trail the rest until the first close below the 10-day (or 20-day) average.
2. **Episodic Pivot (EP)**
   - **What it is:** a surprise catalyst, mainly earnings and guidance.
   - **Rules:**
     - a **gap up of 10%+** on **big volume** (often a full day's average volume in the first 15–30 minutes);
     - strong growth and a big beat;
     - ideally a stock that **hadn't already rallied** for 3–6 months.
   - **Trading it:** entry above the opening-range high, stop at the day's low, trail with the 10- or 20-day average. He says it takes 4–8 earnings seasons to learn.
3. **Parabolic short** (rarely long)
   - **Setup:** a stock up 50–100%+ in days or weeks (300–1,000%+ for small caps), up 3–5+ days in a row.
   - **Trading it:** short the opening-range low or a failed bounce at VWAP, stop at the day's high, target the 10- and 20-day averages.
   - He calls it **the riskiest** of the three. **The owner can't short** (cash-only), so this one doesn't apply.

## Notable from his comment replies
- **On options:** he avoids them. They add complexity to an already hard job, and for every successful options trader he knows of there are ~50 stock traders.
- **On margin:** "if you have to ask you don't get to use it."
- **On beginners:** "decrease size until you get confidence," and "education, education."

## Is he legit?
- **As a person: largely yes.**
  - A long public history.
  - Free content, no paid course.
  - Unusually checkable income through Swedish tax records.
  - Admits blow-ups and drawdowns.
  - Respected across the trading community.
- **As a strategy for the owner: unproven, and harder than it looks.**
  - **Hand-picked examples:** every chart in the post (TSLA 2020, NVDA 2016–17, MNKD, TASR, FSLR) is a famous winner. His own Evernote collection is of setups that worked, which is survivorship bias.
  - **He stresses it takes years** of daily study and execution skill. It also clearly works best in bull markets, and his reported 2022 giveback shows the downside.
  - **One famous success doesn't mean the method works for most followers.** Thousands copy these setups.

## How it connects to our own tests
- **Breakouts = our test #3** (Sept 29 TikTok video 1, the same style). It **failed**: profit factor 0.94, −18% vs SPY +336%, 2016–2026.
  - **Our version differed in places:** it entered at the close (not the opening-range high), used "top 30% by 21-day return" (not his top 1–2% over 1, 3 and 6 months), and didn't cap the stop at the average daily range.
  - A fairer, closer replication would need intraday entries. That's a possible future test, but test #3's failure lowers expectations.
- **The EP (earnings gap-up) is the one with real independent evidence.**
  - Academic research has long found that stocks with big positive earnings surprises, and large gap-ups on earnings, tend to keep drifting up for weeks ("post-earnings announcement drift").
  - **It's the most promising of his three to test properly,** with our 5,259-stock daily data plus earnings dates. It's long-only, so it fits a cash account.
