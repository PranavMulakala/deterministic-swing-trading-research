# Three YouTube videos (sent Oct 5, 2026): what they say, and is anything useful?

- **Sources:** the owner's pasted transcripts, plus each video's page and description (read in the browser Oct 5).
- Summaries are in my own words; no transcripts are stored. Evidence only, not advice.

## 1. JackTrades: "I traded this strategy every day for 3 months and didn't lose once" (170K views, about 2 weeks old)
**What he teaches: "Power of 3" (PO3), an ICT idea.**
- **The idea:** price moves in three steps.
  - **Accumulation:** it moves sideways.
  - **Manipulation:** a fake-out stops people out.
  - **Distribution:** the real move.
- **His setup (long side):**
  - Line it up with the 4-hour candle that opens at 10:00 AM ET.
  - Wait for sideways price, then a dip into the nearest 15-minute "fair value gap" that becomes that 4-hour candle's low.
  - Wait for a 1-minute confirmation: a bearish gap broken, then a new bullish gap that holds.
  - Buy. Stop at that low; target 1–2× the risk.
- **His prop-firm method:** once an account has a cushion, he takes tiny wins (often 0.5×, sometimes 0.3× the risk) just to log "winning days" for payouts.

**Honest take:**
- **The "never lost a day" claim comes from those tiny targets, not from the setup.** Taking profit at 0.3–0.5× the risk makes winning days likely, but each loss is 2–3 winners. That's the pattern our tests #1, #9, #10 and #11 showed: a high win rate with no edge.
- **It's one account.** He says he runs groups of prop accounts. Showing the one that never lost is survivorship.
- **Payouts aren't profit.** "$40k of payouts in 30 days" leaves out the evaluation fees on all the accounts.
- **PO3 is the same family as CRT ("sweep and reclaim"),** which we tested twice (#8, #9). Both lost money.
- **It's a sales funnel:** the description leads to a "free 6-figure payout workshop", and the video to a paid one-on-one mentorship with "guaranteed payout months".
- **The one useful piece:** his win-rate vs reward/risk chart is correct math. The break-even win rate is 1 ÷ (1 + reward/risk).

**Verdict:** skip. Nothing new to test.

## 2. Trade Your Edge (Steven): "How to Trade the First 5 Minutes (And Be Done by 10 AM)" (235K views, about 7 months old)
**What he teaches: the "fence bar", on QQQ 5-minute bars.**
1. Mark the high and low of the first 5-minute candle (9:30–9:35). That's the fence.
2. Wait for a 5-minute candle that **closes outside** the fence.
3. Wait for a **retest candle**: it starts outside, dips back into the fence, and closes outside. Enter at its close.
4. Put the stop about halfway into the fence (or just past nearby support). The target is **2× the risk**.
5. **Context:** a 20-period average (his "anchor line"). He prefers it rising for longs (or flat), and takes profit sooner when price is far from it.

**Honest take:**
- **The clearest rules of the three:** mechanical, with a defined stop and target.
- **His proof is 4 wins from 4 triggers over 5 recent days.** That's far too few to mean anything.
- **It's an opening-range breakout with a retest.** Our own ORB research (September) was no-go, and published ORB results mostly fail once costs are counted.
- **The indicator link is a Flux Charts "creator" link,** which usually earns him a commission. Flux Charts sells TradingView indicators for about $60–120 a month.
- **His credentials are specific and checkable:** 30 years in markets, ex-market maker, Series 57 exam committee. More credible than most.

**Verdict: worth one pre-declared test.** It's fully mechanical, and our QQQ/SPY 5-minute data (2016–2026) is already on disk, so it costs nothing.
