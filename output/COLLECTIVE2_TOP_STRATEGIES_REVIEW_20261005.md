- **Source:** collective2.com/leader-board ("Popular, all instruments") and each strategy's public detail page, read in the browser on Oct 5. Data, not advice.
- **Collective2's own banner:** "All performance results are hypothetical." It tracks each strategy forward from launch and uses AutoTraders' real fill prices, with the subscription fee and commissions included. Track records are still not audited accounts.

## What the headline numbers hide
- **ETF Timer's +16.1%** comes mostly from +77% (2008) and +75% (2009). **From 2010–2025 it compounds to about 9.5%/yr,** below SPY (~13.8%). It also sat in cash for most of 2021–22.
- **extreme-os's +21.8%** comes from +135% and +124% in 2005–06. **2016–2025: about 8.3%/yr** (SPY ~14.8%), with losing years in 2022 and 2024.
- **Fearless Value:** most of its record is one year (**+812% in 2020**). It lost 28% and 9% in 2018–19, and its worst drop was −65%.
- **UnicornIndex:** −64% in 2022 and a −78% worst drop. **AI TQQQ:** −38% in 2022, −51% worst drop.
- **Several have no live AutoTraders listed,** so there's no evidence anyone actually follows them with real money.

## The one that stands out: ares
- **What it does** (per its page): trades **MNQ** (micro Nasdaq futures) long and short, intraday only, never overnight. It risks 1% of the account per trade, always uses a stop, and is 100% automated. Its style is short-term trend-following.
- **Record:**
  - **Years:** 2021 +17.8%, 2022 +76.4%, 2023 +17.8%, 2024 +70.1%, 2025 +5.0%, 2026 to date +12.1%.
  - **Growth:** full years 2021–25 compound to about **34%/yr**, vs SPY about 15%.
  - **Worst drop:** −14%.
  - **Trades:** 36% win rate, profit factor 1.5. Few winners, but bigger than the losers.
- **Why it's more credible than the rest:** 72 live AutoTraders with $9.9M following, a 6-year record, positive every year, a small worst drop, and clear rules similar to what the owner's own MNQ paper test does.
- **Cautions:**
  - Results are still labelled hypothetical, and returns were lumpy (two big years, 2022 and 2024; only +5% in 2025).
  - **The developer has capped subscribers,** so it may not be open.
  - **AutoTrade needs a supported futures broker** (Collective2 lists IBKR, Tradier, AMP and others). Robinhood isn't among the ones shown.
  - **Futures carry leverage.**
  - $100/mo ($1,200/yr) plus per-trade AutoTrade fees.

## Verdict
- **Most of the leaderboard isn't viable:** young records, returns that come from one lucky year, very deep drops, or no real followers.
- **ares is the only one worth a closer look.** If the owner wants to pursue it, the sensible path is to watch it free on Collective2 ("Simulate this strategy" is free) for 1–3 months. Record whether its live results keep matching the claims before paying or auto-following real money.
