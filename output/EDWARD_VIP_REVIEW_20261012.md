# EdwardAlerts VIP review (October 12, 2026)

- **What was read:** the owner paid about $60 for VIP access and logged in themselves.
  - All of VIP **#alerts** from Sept 4 to Oct 9 (125 posts; **26 option alerts** from Sept 9 to Oct 9).
  - VIP **#chat** from Sept 20 to Oct 11 (1,115 messages).
  - Read only, via Discord search.
- **Prices:** every alert was checked against Alpaca OPRA minute bars.
- Raw messages stay out of git (in gitignored `outputs/edward_alerts/`). Research, not advice.

## How VIP differs from the free server
| | Free #trade-ideas | VIP #alerts |
|---|---|---|
| Alerts (similar period) | 7 | **26** |
| Exit guidance | none | **"When I post % gain, that's a good place to trim — that means I'm trimming"**; "trim most," "out majority," "stopped out," "SOLD MAJORITY" |

**The big winners:** COIN 0DTE (to +600%), SPY 777C 10/6 and 10/7 (to +400%), PLTR 0DTE (to +366%), MSM (+200%), IDA (+115%), IBM (+130%).

**The losers, which he mostly didn't follow up in #alerts:**

## Verdict
- **VIP is better than free in one real way:** you get more alerts and actual trim calls.
- **Under any rule you could follow live, it still lost money over these 26 alerts:** −$981 to −$1,919 on $300 each.
- **It only made money if you sold runners at his final post,** which you know only afterward.
- **Members do best by taking quick 20–30% profits.** That fits the data: early trims capture little, and the losers go to zero.
- Keep scoring for 30+ alerts before trusting it with real size.
