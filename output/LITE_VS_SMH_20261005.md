# LITE vs SMH, and whether a "more profitable LITE" exists (October 5, 2026)

- **Data:** Alpaca SIP daily closes (dividends included), to the Oct 5, 2026 close.
- **Code:** `research/lite_like_screener_v1/lite_vs_smh.py` → `output/lite-vs-smh-20261005.json`.
- **Hindsight, descriptive only:** not a forecast or a buy list.
- **Main view: post-COVID** (from Jan 2, 2020, the owner's default window from today). The full history since 2016 is shown as the stress check.

## Post-COVID: $10,000 from Jan 2, 2020 (worst drop along the way in brackets)
| Window | LITE | SMH (chip ETF) | MU | SPY |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Jan 2020 → now** | **$137,700** (−67%) | $93,000 (−45%) | **$202,900** (−58%) | $26,500 (−34%) |
| Jan 2020 → end of 2024 (5 years) | $10,600 (−67%) | **$35,400** (−45%) | $16,000 (−50%) | $19,600 (−34%) |
| 2025 → now (21 months) | **$130,000** (−51%) | $26,300 (−33%) | $126,900 (−41%) | $13,500 (−19%) |

- **For 5 of the last 6¾ years, LITE went nowhere.** It was +6% in total by the end of 2024, after falling 67% in 2021–23. SMH tripled over the same years. Even SPY did better.
- **All of LITE's lead comes from the last 21 months.**
- **Growth per unit of pain** (yearly growth ÷ worst drop): SMH 0.86 vs LITE 0.71. Even in this window, SMH paid more for each point of drop.

## What this means
- **"More profitable than LITE" only shows up looking back.** Most of the bigger winners had 80–96% drops on the way. Holding through those is nearly impossible in practice.
- **MU, which you own, beat LITE since 2020 with a smaller worst drop.**
- **SMH is the steadier way to own the whole chip theme:** it beat LITE most years and has never fallen as far.
- **A fair way to look for the next one is a pre-declared test of a selection rule,** for example "hold the 3–5 strongest LITE-like stocks, re-picked monthly, vs SMH". Not a name picked from this table.
