## What this means
- **"More profitable than LITE" only shows up looking back.** The winners are the same AI bet, with even deeper drops. The same "volatile" group also produced 90%+ wipeouts.
- **MU, which you already own, beat LITE over 5 years and since 2016:** $10k → $761,600 since 2016, worst drop −58%.
- **SMH is the "boring" way to hold the whole chip theme.** It trailed LITE badly in 2025–26, but beat it for most of the decade with far smaller drops.
- **A fair way to look for the next one is a pre-declared test of a selection rule,** for example "hold the 3–5 strongest LITE-like stocks, re-picked monthly, vs SMH". Not a hand-picked name from this table.
