# "Demon Alerts" (Optionality Discord) review: Sept 18 – Oct 9, 2026 (October 11, 2026)

- **What was read:** the owner opened the paid "Optionality" server in the Claude Browser pane.
  - All of #demon-alerts for three weeks (175 messages, Sept 16 – Oct 9), through Discord search.
  - Every screenshot he posted in that window (35 images), plus the Pro upgrade channel.
  - Read only.
- **Prices:** all **30 buy alerts** were checked against Alpaca OPRA minute bars.
- **Research, not advice.** Raw messages are kept only in gitignored `outputs/demon_alerts/`.

## What he does
- **Style:** mostly **same-day (0DTE) options** on SPY, QQQ, TSLA, NVDA, MSFT, COIN and QCOM. He posts "BTO $SPY 772c 10/05 @0.75," often with "NO STOP LOSS."
- **Swings:** a few 2–3-week calls on small caps (LOAR, INTC, BCO, ASTE, DBD, ESNT).
- **Updates:** "$SPY @2.8 from 0.45 | +522%," "BANGGG," "BACK TO BACK," "NOBODY SHOULD LOSE ON THIS," "TAG ME IN PROFITS."
- **Averaging down:** sometimes, e.g. "Brought my avg to 0.80."
- **Exits:** rarely told; it's usually "holding runners."
- **The business:**
  - He's one of ~15 alert channels in **"Optionality Pro"**, a paid tier sold at `0ptions.com/upgrade`. The domain starts with a zero, not an O.
  - The server also has a **referral program**.
  - He warns about impersonators DMing for money.

## His screenshots
- **All 35 are cropped green strips:** ticker plus a % gain (e.g. "COIN Sep18'26 190 Call 627.0%," "QQQ Oct08'26 754… 985.4%").
- **None shows dollars, contract count, fill price or an exit.**
- **Every one is a winner.** There's no screenshot for any of the 18 alerts that lost.

## Is the alert price real?
- **Usually not what you could get.** At the minute he posted, the option was already **above** his quoted price in most cases (e.g. SPY 770c posted 0.45, trading 0.53; QCOM posted 0.80, then 0.85 → 1.17).
- **A follower buying in the next minute paid on average 19% more than his posted price,** up to +50%.
- **The %s in his updates are from his own earlier and sometimes averaged-down fill,** not yours.

## All 30 alerts as a follower ($450 per alert, about 10% of a $4,500 account)
**Entry:** the high of the minute after the alert.

| Exit rule | Total over 30 alerts | Winners |
|---|---|---|
| **Sell exactly at his last brag/screenshot** (hindsight: he never says it's the last) | **+$7,642** | 12/30 |
| Hold everything (no exit) | **−$3,128** | 5/30 |
| Your own +30% limit | −$4,337 | 15/30 |
| Your own +50% limit | −$4,982 | 12/30 |
| Your own +100% limit | −$5,088 | 9/30 |
| Trailing exit (after +50%, sell on a 30% pullback) | −$5,652 | 12/30 |

**Growing the account instead (10% of the balance per alert):**

| Exit rule | $4,500 becomes |
|---|---|
| His last brag | $14,962 |
| +50% limit | $1,692 |
| Hold | $1,887 |

**Where the money is:**
- **The "+$7,642" comes almost entirely from 4 trades** held for hours: COIN +504%, SPY 770c +671%, SPY 772c +387%, QQQ 754p +775%.
- **To get it, you have to hold a 0DTE call through big swings and sell at the exact moment he posts his final screenshot.** That moment is only known afterward.
- **Any rule you can actually follow lost money:**
  - Taking profit early caps the big winners while the losers still go to −100%.
  - Trailing stops get shaken out of the big winners early (COIN +21% instead of +504%).
- **The losers he never mentioned again:** 8 swing calls (INTC, BCO, DBD, ASTE, ESNT, LOAR, NFLX, MSFT 510c), all −78% to −100%. A few same-day trades were admitted as stopped out or "dead."

## Verdict
- **The trades happen.** The contracts exist and moved roughly as described on the winners.
- **The presentation is one-sided:**
  - posted prices are better than a follower can get;
  - only winners are screenshotted, as %s;
  - losers aren't followed up;
  - the hype ("NOBODY SHOULD LOSE") sells the paid tier.
- **For a $4–5k follower over these 3 weeks:**
  - With any repeatable exit, about **−$3,000 to −$5,000**. That's most or all of the account, and fixed $450 bets would have run it out before the 30th alert.
  - Only perfect hindsight selling at his final brag made money.
- **Same pattern as OWLS (test #28) and EdwardAlerts:** what's posted looks far better than what a follower gets.
