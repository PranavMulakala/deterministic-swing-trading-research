> **My Roth plan: 80% VT + 20% AVUV**
> - Invest the $5,967 now: about $4,774 in VT and $1,193 in AVUV.
> - Recurring every 2 weeks: $200 VT + $50 AVUV. If I raise it to $289 to max the $7,500 limit: $231 + $58.
> - Turn on dividend reinvestment.
> - Once a year, if AVUV has drifted outside 15–25%, send new money to whichever side is behind. No selling.
> - Never buy VTI, VXUS, LITE or MU inside the Roth.
>
> **Why this plan (from my own research, data through Sep 2026)**
> 1. **VT instead of VTI/VXUS:** my taxable account holds VTI and VXUS. Buying them every 2 weeks in the Roth
>    would turn any tax-loss sale in taxable into a wash sale, and when the IRA does the buying the deduction is
>    lost forever (Rev. Rul. 2008-5). VT tracks a different index. It costs 0.06% vs about 0.04%, roughly
>    $2.5k less by age 55.
> 2. **No growth tilt (QQQM, SPMO, SMH):** with LITE and MU I already have about 50%+ of my stocks in tech.
>    SPMO's #1 holding is MU (9.5%), and QQQM and SMH hold about 5% MU each.
> 3. **AVUV (US small-cap value, 0.25%):** it holds no LITE or MU and only 5% tech. I tested it against rules I
>    wrote down before running it, and it passed all three:
>    - forward-model median at 55: $575k vs $564k for VT alone;
>    - if small value has no edge, the cost is only about −0.1% (~$500);
>    - its worst dollar drop since 2008 was −$49.7k vs −$52.7k for VT.
>    - **Catch:** it fell 10.7% behind plain VT from Dec 2016 to Mar 2020 and only caught up in Oct 2022.
>

> - Should I raise contributions to $289 to max the Roth?
