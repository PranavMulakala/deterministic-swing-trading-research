# How MU moved after Samsung's preliminary results (descriptive, Oct 7, 2026)

Information only: not a pre-declared test, and not advice.
- **Data:** 31 Samsung preliminary reports (Q4 2018 – Q2 2026). The dates and beat/miss (±3% vs consensus, mostly FnGuide) were gathered from Samsung's newsroom and Korean and English press; sources are in the session notes.
- **MU prices:** Alpaca daily bars. The US session that follows the ~8–9 AM KST release counts as the reaction day. Apr 7, 2023 was Good Friday, so Apr 10 is used.
- **Code:** `research/samsung_mu_v1/` (`study.py`, `samsung_prelim_dates.json`).

| Samsung result | Reports | MU closed up | Average MU day | Average vs the chip ETF (SMH) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **All** | 31 | 9 (29%) | −0.70% | −0.42% |
| **Beat** | 17 | **3 (18%)** | **−1.67%** | −1.01% |
| Miss | 11 | 5 (45%) | +0.84% | +0.45% |
| In line | 3 | 1 | −0.79% | −0.24% |

- **These days are not bigger than a normal MU day:** the average absolute move was 2.29%, vs 2.45% on all days since Dec 2018.
- **The last three big beats:**
  - Oct 2025: MU −3.0%;
  - Jan 2026: −3.7%;
  - Jul 2026: −4.7% (it opened −6.3%).
  - Apr 2026 (a huge beat): flat.
- **Misses sometimes lifted MU.** Apr 2023: Samsung's miss came with production cuts, and MU rose +8.0%.
- **Caution:**
  - 31 events, several ways of slicing them, and nothing pre-declared.
  - "Beats → MU down" (t ≈ −2) is a hint, not a rule.
  - Most of MU's move on these days follows the whole chip sector and the US market, not Samsung alone.
