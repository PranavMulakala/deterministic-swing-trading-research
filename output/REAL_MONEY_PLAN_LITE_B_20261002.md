# My real-money LITE plan, option B: small size, starting week of Oct 5, 2026

**My decision.** I place every order myself in Robinhood. Claude never places real-money orders. This plan is not investment advice; it's the rule I chose, written down while calm.

## The rule (the same one the paper bot uses)
1. **Signal:** at 10:00 AM ET, LITE dipped at least **1% below its 9:30 open** in the first 30 minutes **and** is still below the open at 9:55.
   - The paper bot checks this and emails me at about 10:00–10:01. An email with **"Signal" / "Filled And Target Placed"** means the rule fired; **"No Signal"** means it didn't.
2. **Buy:** a **limit buy** of my share count at about the current price + 0.1%, in **regular market hours**, good for the day. If it doesn't fill by 10:05, I cancel it. No chasing.
3. **Sell:** as soon as it fills, a **limit sell, good till canceled**, at **my fill price × 1.02** (+2%). Optionally +3%.
4. **One position at a time.** If I'm already holding, I skip new signals, the same as the bot.
5. **No automatic stop.** My −20% check comes from my sell note: I decide that evening.

## Money rules
- **Cash only, no margin** (my dad's company policy, and good sense).
- **After each sale, wait for settlement** (next business day) before using that money to buy LITE again. That avoids "good faith violations".
- **Taxes:** set aside about 29% of each profit.
- **Keep the rest of my cash separate** and untouched by this plan.

## What I do each trading day (about 5 minutes)
1. **10:00–10:01:** read the paper bot's email.
2. **If it says Signal and I'm not holding:**
   - place the limit buy;
   - when it fills, place the +2% limit sell;
   - send Claude my fill price and share count.
3. **If I'm holding:** do nothing unless LITE closed −20% or more below my buy (then follow my sell note).
4. **After 4 PM:** glance at the close. Don't watch all day.

## When I change the size: decided now, not in the moment
- **Scale up (for example to 45 shares) only if all of these hold after 3 weeks:**
  - I followed the rule every time (no skipped signals, no extra trades);
  - my real results match the paper bot's within about 0.5% per trade;
  - no position is down 10% or more.
- **Pause and rethink if any of these happens:**
  - a position closes −20% below my buy;
  - 6 weeks pass with no sale;
  - I break a rule twice.

## Things to remember
- **One paper trade isn't proof.** The replay's best year (2026) is the year the rule was chosen from.
- **Days that look obvious afterwards** (like Oct 2: $1,037 → $1,085) aren't signals. The rule decides, not my eyes.
