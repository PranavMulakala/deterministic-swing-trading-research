The 3 ways money gets made

Think of a lemonade stand on your street.

Engine 1
Own the stand

You buy part of a business. If it grows, your part is worth more. That's buying shares and holding them.

Your LITE and MU shares

Engine 2
Sell rain insurance

Other stands pay you a small fee every week to promise you'll help if it storms. Most weeks you keep the fee. Some weeks it storms hard and you pay out a lot.

Selling puts and calls · the wheel

Engine 3
Guess tomorrow's weather

You bet on what happens next: up or down, today or this week. Being right a little more than half the time isn't enough after costs.

Day trading · 0DTE · chart patterns · alerts

Most "$20k in options" screenshots are Engine 3 with borrowed money. The big wins get posted and the losses don't. In our OWLS check, the analyst posted +142% while a follower copying him got +49%.

Words you need (8 of them)

Share
A tiny piece of a company. 1 LITE share ≈ $1,103.
Option
A contract about 100 shares. It's a promise, not the shares themselves.
Call
The right to buy 100 shares at a set price. If you sell one, you promise to sell your shares at that price.
Put
The right to sell 100 shares at a set price. If you sell one, you promise to buy 100 shares at that price if asked.
Strike
The set price in the promise.
Expiration
The day the promise ends. "Weekly" = about 7 days.
Premium
The fee paid for the promise. When you sell, you get it right away and keep it.
Delta
Roughly the chance the promise gets used. A 0.30-delta put ≈ a 30% chance you end up buying the shares.

B · Sell puts on LITE or MU $1k+/wk, but deep drops

How to start (instead of 30 ideas at once)

  1. Pick one playbook for 8 weeks. Plan B, or the wheel if tonight's tests hold up.
  2. Write the rules on one card: when you get in, when you take profit, what you do if it drops, how much per trade.
  3. Paper first, or small with real money. Account U036 is ready for option practice.
  4. Log dollars every week: what it made, and the worst moment.
  5. Sunday check-in, 15 minutes: did you follow the rules? New ideas go on a "later" list.
