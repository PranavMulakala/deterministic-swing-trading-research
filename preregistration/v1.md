# Pre-registration: ETF Pullback Uptrend v1

Status: **REGISTERED — PAPER TRADING DISABLED**

Registered: 2026-09-05, before any paper order submission and before the first
backtest run of this implementation.

## Frozen hypothesis and parameters

The hypothesis is that a very short-term oversold pullback can mean-revert while
an ETF remains in a long-term uptrend.

- Static universe: SPY, QQQ, IWM, DIA, XLB, XLE, XLF, XLI, XLK, XLP, XLRE,
  XLU, XLV, XLY, XLC.
- Long only; cash only; no leverage, shorting, options, crypto, or leveraged ETFs.
- Signal after a completed daily bar: close above SMA(200) and Wilder RSI(2) below 10.
- Entry: next available daily open.
- Recovery exit signal: completed daily close at or above SMA(20); exit next open.
- Protective stop: entry reference price minus 2.0 times signal-day Wilder ATR(14).
- Time exit signal: after 20 trading sessions in the position; exit next open.
- First exit condition wins. A gap through the stop fills at the next open with
  modeled sell-side spread, slippage, and fees.
- Planned loss per trade: at most 0.5% of then-current equity.
- Aggregate planned open risk: at most 3% of then-current equity.
- Daily realized-loss halt: 2% of start-of-day equity.
- Costs per side: 1 bp half-spread, 2 bp slippage, 0.1 bp fee.
- Initial equity: $500; fractional shares rounded down to six decimals.
- No parameter sweep or optimization is permitted for v1.

The machine-readable authority is `config/strategy_v1.json`. Its SHA-256 is
recorded in `config/strategy_v1.sha256` before the first backtest is run.

## Chronological evaluation

- Data begins 2006-01-01, subject to each ETF's actual history.
- In-sample period ends 2020-12-31.
- Out-of-sample period begins 2021-01-01 and ends 2026-08-31.
- Pre-2021 observations may warm indicators but cannot create an out-of-sample
  trade before 2021-01-01.
- The same frozen parameter set is reported separately in-sample and
  out-of-sample. No result may be used to revise v1; any revision is v2 and
  requires a new pre-registration and a new untouched evaluation period.

## Milestone 1 acceptance criteria

- One command reproduces tests, metrics, trades, equity, exposure, ledger,
  dashboard, and report from pinned input files.
- Identical inputs and config produce byte-identical output artifacts.
- Zero same-bar entries or discretionary fills.
- Every accepted trade passed the isolated risk module.
- Every trade records planned risk dollars and percent against the 0.5% limit.
- SPY buy-and-hold and the frozen momentum baseline use the same period and cost model.
- No AI/LLM dependency or call exists in selection, signal, sizing, risk,
  backtest, or order-planning paths.

## Gate for later tiny-real-money consideration

All conditions are required; passing them is not automatic authorization:

1. At least 63 completed US trading sessions (approximately three months) of
   Alpaca paper operation after validator approval.
2. Every broker order, fill, cash movement, position, and protective stop
   reconciles daily with the local append-only ledger.
3. Zero unexplained accounting discrepancies.
4. Zero risk-limit breaches caused by software or configuration.
5. Zero unprotected open positions: every entry has a broker-acknowledged native stop.
6. Paper maximum drawdown does not exceed 10%.
7. Paper return is not more than 10 percentage points below SPY over the same
   period, and observed trade frequency, win rate, average win/loss, and
   slippage each lie within the central 95% bootstrap interval of the frozen
   out-of-sample backtest.
8. Owner and independent validator both approve the exact code revision and
   deployment checklist. Real execution remains disabled until that approval.

## Kill and halt criteria

Any one condition immediately halts new paper orders and requires investigation:

- Any risk-limit breach caused by code, including one-cent tolerance breaches.
- Any position exists at Alpaca without a broker-acknowledged protective stop.
- Any broker/local position or cash mismatch remains unexplained after the next
  reconciliation cycle or 15 minutes, whichever comes first.
- Any duplicate client order identifier or duplicate economic order is accepted.
- Any execution occurs while the global kill switch is active.
- Paper peak-to-trough drawdown reaches 10%.
- Over any completed rolling 42-session window after the initial 63 sessions,
  strategy return trails SPY by 10 percentage points or more.
- Three consecutive trading sessions have missing, stale, or unreconciled data.

Abandon v1 rather than merely pause it if two independent validator audits find
the same unresolved look-ahead, accounting, or risk-bypass defect, or if the
42-session relative-underperformance kill criterion fires twice.
