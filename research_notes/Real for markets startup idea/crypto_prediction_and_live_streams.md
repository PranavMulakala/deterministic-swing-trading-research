## 1. pump.fun: what a coin page contains, user and revenue scale, livestream controversies and moderation, 2025–2026 developments

### Takeaway
pump.fun is the clearest proof that putting a live price, a live trade tape, a comment thread and a livestream on one asset page can generate huge engagement and revenue. It has earned about $1.16B in cumulative revenue and about $1.6–1.9M per day in early October 2026. Its social layer has also been a steady source of harm. About 98.6% of its tokens collapse into pump-and-dumps. Livestreams were suspended after self-harm and animal-cruelty stunts. It faces a RICO class action. The money comes from trading fees on every trade. The social features exist to bring people in and keep them trading.

### Cited Findings
#### Product mechanics (coin page / launch flow)
- Launching a token requires a name, ticker, description and photo, with optional X/Telegram/website links. Each token has a bonding curve that fills as market cap rises. It "graduates" at a set market cap (about $66k when the article was written) and then trades on PumpSwap. Creating a token has been free since Aug 2024 (it used to cost 0.02 SOL). pump.fun "has a built-in livestream feature" — [Decrypt (updated Jul 18, 2025)](https://decrypt.co/247825/what-is-pump-fun-the-solana-meme-coin-factory/)
- Graduating tokens originally moved to Raydium. From Mar 20, 2025 they move to pump.fun's own AMM, PumpSwap — [Blockworks analytics](https://blockworks.co/analytics/pumpfun/pumpfun-tradeactvity)
- Third-party guides tell traders to watch market cap, holder count/distribution and the buy/sell ratio before graduation. That suggests holder and trade-flow data are core page elements — [skills.cat pump.fun mechanics guide](https://skills.cat/skills/sanctifiedops/solana-skills/pump-fun-mechanics)
- In Nov 2024 the co-founder described the moderation workload as "increased scale of coins, streams, and comments". Comments, streams and coins are the three social surfaces — [Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/pump-fun-livestream-backlash-content)

### Inferences
- A pump.fun coin page works like a live game page. The chart is the scoreboard, the trade tape is the play-by-play, the thread is the comments and the livestream is the broadcast. It differs from Real in one way: every spectator can bet on the next play in one tap. That money-on-the-line loop drives the engagement, and it also drives the abuse.
- Paying creators a share of trading volume on their own token (Project Ascend) directly rewarded stunts. Pay-for-attention tied to price is the mechanic behind pump.fun's worst moments. A "Real for markets" product should not link anyone's payout to the price of what they are talking about.
- Revenue is falling quarter over quarter in 2026 despite buybacks, and a rival (LetsBonk) briefly took the lead in 2025. Memecoin engagement is cyclical and hype-driven, not a steady habit.
- pump.fun bought a terminal (Padre) and now earns "Terminal" revenue. That suggests the money is in owning the execution surface where active traders spend their session, not only the launchpad.

### Gaps
- I found no primary or help-doc description of the current coin-page UI and its tabs. From general familiarity (unverified here), a page shows a price chart, a live trades list, a reply thread, holder distribution, bonding-curve progress and an embedded stream when the creator is live. Check this on pump.fun directly.
- No reliable 2026 user/DAU counts or livestream viewer counts. pump.fun does not publish them and Streams Charts does not track it.
- No 2026 change to the livestream policy found. I found no details on "Mayhem Mode" (mentioned only in passing as excluded from dashboard revenue) or on the 2026 status of the RICO case.

## 2. Trading terminals and social trading apps (Axiom, GMGN, Photon, BullX, Fomo, DEX Screener, Telegram bots): social features, speed, scale, revenue, funding

### Takeaway
Three social features clearly drive volume in crypto trading apps: (1) seeing real trades by people you follow as they happen (wallet tracking, live network feeds), (2) PnL leaderboards, and (3) a one-tap way to copy a trade. They sit on top of execution, where speed is the selling point. The money is large and fast. Axiom reached about $100M in revenue roughly 4 months after launch and about $280M in 2025. Fomo, a mobile-first, social-first app, raised $17M (Benchmark, Nov 2025) and then $75M at a $550M valuation (Index, June 2026), and passed Axiom in daily Solana volume in Aug 2026. The category is also unstable and has trust problems. BullX paused trading in June 2026, Axiom faced insider front-running allegations, GMGN users were hit by MEV attacks, and Banana Gun was exploited.

### Inferences
- The social feature that works is **verified, real-money activity from people you chose to follow, shown live**. Crypto gets it for free because wallets are public on-chain. Stocks and options need broker-linked verification, which is the approach Robinhood Social takes (see Q4).
- Speed matters in terminals because they execute trades, and in memecoins latency is edge. A "Real for markets" that doesn't execute competes on **how fresh the data is and how fast notifications arrive**, like Real's fast scores, not on order routing.
- Fomo's rise shows a clean, mobile, social-feed product can take share from power-user terminals in about 15 months. That supports the "clean, simple UI" thesis. Fomo also says it is heading toward tokenized stocks and prediction markets, so crypto-native social apps may become competitors for a stocks product.
- Growth runs on referral and affiliate payouts (up to 30% rebates, and net revenue about half of gross). That creates a "KOL call" culture, which spreads the app but also degrades the quality of signals.
- Trust failures repeat across the category: insider access to user wallet data, MEV, exploits and sudden shutdowns. A social app that holds users' position data must treat that data as sensitive from day one.

## 3. Base App, Zora, Farcaster, friend.tech: what worked or failed in combining social and markets

### Takeaway
Every attempt to make the social object itself tradable has spiked and then collapsed. friend.tech sold "keys" to people. Zora and Base App turned posts and creators into coins. Coinbase's CEO said of Base App's social-coin push: "It didn't work. We pivoted earlier this year." Farcaster, the crypto-native Twitter, stayed small and changed owners twice in 2026. What lasted is social around trading. Base App's feed now centers on tradable assets and on-chain activity.

### Cited Findings
- **Base App launch:** In July 2025 Coinbase rebranded Coinbase Wallet as the Base App, combining a social feed, chat, payments, trading and app discovery. It launched in 140+ countries in Dec 2025 — [Odaily/Foresight News (Jul 14, 2026)](https://www.odaily.news/en/post/5211890); [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/362713/coinbase-unveils-base-app-rebrands-wallet-as-all-in-one-social-and-trading-platform)
- **Zora mechanics:** every post became an ERC-20 "content coin" with a 1B supply, and the creator got 1%. Each account also had one "creator coin", 50% on the market and 50% vesting to the creator over 5 years — [Odaily](https://www.odaily.news/en/post/5211890)
- **Spike:** in August 2025 there were more than 1.6M creator coins minted, nearly 3M unique traders and more than $470M of volume (Dune), and Base passed Solana in token creation — [CoinDesk (Aug 3, 2025)](https://www.coindesk.com/tech/2025/08/03/base-takes-solana-s-crown-in-token-creation-as-coinbase-s-socialfi-ignites-zora-boom). Zora was called a "hypercasino hellscape" after a 600% price surge — [DL News](https://www.dlnews.com/articles/defi/socialfi-project-zora-surges-after-base-app-launch/)
- **Crash:** the ZORA market cap fell from about $550M (Aug 2025 high) to about $30M, roughly -95%. The "Base is for everyone" coin auto-created from an official Base post (Apr 2025) fell about 95% within hours — [Odaily](https://www.odaily.news/en/post/5211890)
- **Pivot:** in Feb 2026 Base App removed its Farcaster-powered "Talk" social feed. "The in-app feed now centers entirely on tradable assets and onchain activity." Base Creator Rewards wound down after more than $450k was paid to about 17,000 creators over six months. ## 5. Live trading streams (Twitch, YouTube, Kick, X Spaces): who streams the open or crypto trading, audience sizes, platform rules

Live market streaming is real but small on Twitch: about 3k average viewers across roughly 90 live channels in Twitch's Crypto category in mid-2026. Dedicated stock-market shows run daily on YouTube. ## 6. What this says about the founder's idea: is "the market as a live game" already a working consumer behavior, and where is the gap for stocks and options?

### Takeaway
Yes, outside traditional stocks the "live game plus comment on every move" behavior is proven at scale. pump.fun coin pages show trades and comments live. Fomo's feed shows your network's trades. Kalshi has per-event Live Chat. Prediction-market volume peaks around live sports. In crypto and prediction markets the money comes from execution fees, and the worst failures came from making social content tradable or paying people per trade volume. For stocks and options, incumbents are moving in: Robinhood Social (verified trades, live to all US users Oct 2026), X Smart Cashtags (live prices plus posts per ticker, Apr 2026) and Kalshi's financial and crypto markets. As of Oct 2026, nobody seems to own an event-scoped, real-time "game room" for scheduled stock and options moments that works across brokers and shows verified positions. That looks like the wedge, but it is narrow and contested.

### Inferences
- **Proven, worth copying:**
  - An asset or event page as the "game page": live price, live tape and a reaction thread together.
  - Event-scoped live chat with short messages (Kalshi caps at 300 characters).
  - **Position-verified identity**: Kalshi posts attach positions and Robinhood posts verified trades.
  - Follow and notify-on-move (Kalshi, GMGN, Fomo).
  - PnL leaderboards over fixed windows.
  - Shareable cards that embed a chart (Kalshi → Threads).
  - Mobile-first, very simple UI (Fomo beating Axiom).
- **Proven, avoid:**
  - Making posts or creators tradable.
  - Paying creators by trading volume or price.
  - Live video where the streamer profits when the price moves.
  - Unmoderated links in comments.
  - Treating moderation as something to add later. pump.fun and Farcaster both had to rebuild rules after the fact, and Farcaster's retroactive bans caused backlash.
- **Where the stocks/options gap appears to be** (my synthesis, needs validation with users): Robinhood Social is a single-broker feed with no live rooms and no multi-leg options sharing yet. X Cashtags is a noisy, bot-prone post aggregator. Discord alert groups are paid, fragmented and personality-driven. Twitch/YouTube trading streams are one-to-many and mostly small. Nobody yet offers a **Real-style "game" for each scheduled market moment** with:
  - a sub-second tape of "plays" (price breaks, halts, headline drops, unusual options prints, the earnings number hitting the tape),
  - reactions on every play,
  - optional verified-position badges across brokers.
  Possible "games": the 9:30 open, the 3:30–4:00 close, after-hours earnings, the 2:00 FOMC statement, the 8:30 CPI release, 0DTE expiration. Earnings nights look like the best first wedge because they are scheduled, short, dramatic and already discussed live in options Discords.
- **Monetization problem:** every proven model here makes money on execution (pump.fun fees, $1/trade at Fomo, a 1% terminal fee, prediction-market take rates of ~0.3–1.2%). A non-executing "Real for markets" would need broker referral or hand-off revenue (X → Wealthsimple is the template), subscriptions (the Discord-group model) or data/media licensing (Kalshi → CNBC/CNN is the template). Expect incumbents to copy features quickly. Robinhood and X both shipped versions of this in 2026.
- **MVP shape suggested by the evidence** (an inference, not sourced): a web app with no execution, a schedule of "games" (an earnings calendar plus macro events), and one live room per game. The room has a fast quote/tape, auto-generated play-by-play events, emoji and short-text reactions per play, and a lightweight "I'm long/short/flat" stance poll. Self-reported stance can come first, with read-only broker verification later. Strict link blocking, anti-impersonation and rate limits apply from day one. There are no tokens and no creator payouts tied to price.

### Gaps
- There is no direct evidence on whether stock and options traders want many-to-many live rooms rather than feeds. Testing this needs user research, for example with the founder's Discord groups.
- Real-time US equity and OPRA options data licensing costs and latency, which decide whether a "Real-speed" tape is affordable, were not researched here.
- Engagement metrics are missing for Robinhood Social, Kalshi Social/Live Chat and X Cashtags beyond X's claimed $1B in two days.
- I didn't cover Stocktwits, Discord/Whop alert-group economics or the Real app's own metrics. They were outside this assignment, and other researchers may cover them.
