# Frozen protocol

## General

Every evidence packet contains exact strategy/version identity, uppercase symbol, source, `feature_as_of`, `observed_at`, `retrieved_at`, data object and SHA-256 hash of the unsigned canonical JSON. Required ordering is `feature_as_of <= observed_at <= retrieved_at <= decision_at`. Management additionally requires every evidence timestamp and the decision time to be at or after the latest durable fill contributing to the position; equality at that fill-time boundary is valid. Staleness, extra fields, pre-position evidence and hash mismatches reject the packet.

All timestamps are timezone-aware. All money, prices, ratios and quantities use finite decimal representations. Every output is paper-only and has no executable method.

## Stock swing v1

Entry requires: price at least $10; exactly 20 point-in-time dollar-volume observations with median at least $50m; ATR14/price from 3% through 10%; session return from -10% through -2%; close at or above session VWAP and SMA50; no earnings in the next three sessions; no hard-adverse news or broken-thesis flag; and a next executable ask at or below the entry limit. The frozen universe is LITE, COHR, CIEN, FN and AAOI.

The engine sizes fractional simulated shares within 20% of virtual equity. No entry is created when the same symbol is already open. After simulated fills, including partial fills and costs, the durable quantity, basis, opening time, origin reservation and entry-evidence identity are used to construct and revalidate every plan. Caller-supplied quantity, entry price/debit, target, opening time, or evidence hash cannot replace that durable identity. Because the ledger floors cash credits to whole cents, the target first rounds the required 110% cash credit upward to a whole cent, solves for price after exit costs, and rounds the price upward. This applies even to tiny fractional quantities.

A price 10% below basis or reaching 21 sessions without a completed re-underwrite generates `REVIEW_REQUIRED`, not an exit. Explicit `thesis_broken` exits immediately. Missing required re-underwriting at 42 sessions fails closed to a paper exit. Every position exits by 126 sessions. The stock hold clock is derived from the durable fill and decision timestamps using the same conservative UTC-weekday approximation as options; the caller's reported `sessions_held` is diagnostic only. This can exit early on exchange holidays but cannot extend a hold. Averaging down and merging a second reservation into an open position are prohibited.

## Long-option shadow v2

Only one standard 100-multiplier SPY or QQQ long call/put can be open. Expiry is 14-30 calendar days, strike is positive and within 5% of the underlying, open interest is at least 500, volume at least 100, and spread is no more than both $0.05 and 15% of ask. Evidence is at most 30 seconds old.

The deterministic baseline selects a call at a three-bar move of at least +30 bps, a put at -30 bps or less, and otherwise abstains. Council mode needs a hash-bound analyst proposal and an agreeing critic proposal that identify the same instrument, exactly match the evidence packet's underlying/right/expiry/strike, and cite available evidence fields. Missing, malformed, stale, conflicting, ungrounded, substituted-contract, or disagreeing proposals abstain. There are no model calls.

Contractual loss includes one cent premium slippage and a $0.65 fee, is never below reserved premium notional, and may not exceed 5% of account equity. One contract is indivisible. Management uses a fresh, hash-bound `option_management` packet tied to the exact open contract and entry evidence; raw premium arguments are not accepted. Its premium basis, stop, target, open time and entry-evidence hash are derived from and checked against the durable fill and reservation—not trusted from a caller-supplied plan. A zero option mark or close is allowed. Rules are a 30% premium stop, 50% premium target and two-session maximum hold.

The hold clock comes from the durable fill timestamp and current decision timestamp, never the reported `sessions_held` field. Version 2 uses a conservative UTC-weekday approximation because no exchange-calendar adapter exists in this isolated package. If `completed_session_dates` is supplied, it must exactly match that derived sequence. Contract expiration is parsed from the durable contract identity and independently forces a paper exit after otherwise-valid fresh management evidence.

## Risk and broker boundaries

The risk policy caps an equity position at 20%, all deployed capital at 50%, option contractual loss at 5%, daily realized loss at 2%, and high-water drawdown at 5%; it reserves 50% cash. Dates and the durable monotonic account clock use UTC. Each strategy may create at most one new intent per UTC date. Backdated requests, unknown/stale marks, enabled kill switch and accounting conflicts reject state changes. Reservation creation, fill, close, reservation-release and reservation-expiry events have immutable identities and hashes. Reservation creation binds account, strategy/version, symbol, asset type, quantity, notional/loss, evidence identity, frozen policy hash, creation time and expiry time. Identical replays do not change accounting and conflicting reuse rejects. Debits and risk round up to cents; cash credits round down so sub-cent proceeds cannot create phantom value.

Every state-changing admission and every idempotent replay runs reconciliation in the same immediate SQLite transaction before mutating or returning. Reconciliation verifies payload hashes and exact receipt shapes, then chronologically rebuilds cash, position quantities, basis, contractual loss, realized loss, durable origin strategy version, and reservation status/remaining consumption from durable event payloads without trusting materialized position or close economics. Both engines reserve under their literal version and require that same version at replay, plan derivation and management; foreign or unversioned origins fail closed. A mismatch commits and permanently latches an integrity pause even though the attempted admission or replay is rejected. The latch survives reopen and can be cleared only through the explicit repair acknowledgement after event replay is clean; reconciliation never silently clears it. Databases created with an older/incompatible package schema fail closed and require a new database rather than implicit migration.

The controller requires its caller to provide the exact expected virtual account, revalidates evidence freshness/content/hash and decision identity, and hash-seals the resulting card before a receipt can be made. The broker handoff accepts only read-only account, quote and order-review receipts with exact non-empty typed identities and causal timestamps. An order review must be validated alongside the actual signed `QUOTE_READ` receipt and match its hash, symbol and timestamps; a syntactically valid hash alone is rejected. It has status `REVIEW_ONLY`, is not authorization, and cannot be submitted by this package.
